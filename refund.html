Refund Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Every ClickPrompt Pro purchase comes with a 14-day refund window. That covers the monthly plan ($4.99/month), the yearly plan ($49.99/year), and the lifetime license ($129 one-time). If Pro isn't right for you, ask within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. No questions required.

How to ask for a refund

  • Email info@lvltdllc.com from the address you used to buy, with your order number. Your order number is in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.
  • You can also look up your order at Lemon Squeezy My Orders.

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

How refunds are paid

All ClickPrompt sales are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it can take 5 to 10 business days to show up.

What happens after a refund

A refund ends Pro access for that purchase and deactivates its license key. A refunded subscription is also cancelled, so it won't renew. Your saved scripts and voiceovers then follow the same 10-business-day deletion schedule described in our Terms, so you have time to download them.

Renewals

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel any time to stop future charges.

After 14 days

After the 14-day window, charges are generally non-refundable. If you were charged twice, were charged in error, or can't access what you paid for, contact us at any time and we'll sort it out. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under your local consumer protection law.
