Every ClickPrompt Pro purchase comes with a 14-day refund window. That covers the monthly plan ($3.99/month), the yearly plan ($29/year), and the lifetime license ($59 one-time). If Pro isn't right for you, ask within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. No questions required.

How to ask for a refund

  • Email info@lvltdllc.com from the address you used to buy, with your order number. Your order number is in your receipt email (from Lemon Squeezy, or from Stripe if that checkout was used).
  • You can also look up a Lemon Squeezy order at Lemon Squeezy My Orders. For a Stripe purchase, reply to the receipt or email us and we will look it up.

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

How refunds are paid

ClickPrompt sales are processed by the payment provider used at checkout. Today that is Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy. If Stripe checkout is turned on, Stripe is the payment processor for that purchase, Lagniappe Ventures Ltd remains the seller, and approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it can take 5 to 10 business days to show up.

What happens after a refund

A partial refund keeps access. A full refund ends Pro for that purchase. A chargeback pauses Pro until the dispute is resolved. A won dispute, or a warning that closed in your favor (Stripe status warning_closed), restores access. A lost dispute, or any other closed outcome, ends Pro and starts the 10-business-day deletion schedule. For a Lemon Squeezy purchase, a full refund also deactivates the license key. A fully refunded subscription is also cancelled, so it won't renew. Your saved scripts and voiceovers then follow the same 10-business-day deletion schedule described in our Terms, so you have time to download them.

Renewals

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel any time to stop future charges.

After 14 days

After the 14-day window, charges are generally non-refundable. If you were charged twice, were charged in error, or can't access what you paid for, contact us at any time and we'll sort it out. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under your local consumer protection law.
