Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

ClickPrompt is a free teleprompter for recording videos. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can get it deleted.

What we collect

  • Your interview answers. When you generate a script, your answers (name, role, audience, point, length) are sent to our AI provider to draft the script. We do not store your answers after the script is returned.
  • A visitor cookie. We set a first-party cookie (cp_vid) so we can count your 3 free scripts per day. It contains a random ID, nothing about you.
  • IP address. Used only as a fallback for the daily free-script count and for short-term abuse throttling. Not stored long-term.
  • Email address. Only if you subscribe: our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record) shares your email so we can manage your subscription and send cancellation notices.
  • Saved scripts and voiceovers. Only what you explicitly save to your library. Stored so you can pull them up on any device.
  • Anonymous usage counts. Daily totals like "scripts generated" or "recordings started." No personal data, no cross-site tracking.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no passwords, no social logins.
  • No advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies.
  • Your camera and microphone never leave your device — recording happens entirely in your browser.

Who sees your data

Our AI provider (for script generation), our payment provider Lemon Squeezy (for subscriptions), and our hosting provider Cloudflare (which serves the site and stores your saved library). Nobody else. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Deletion

Delete any saved script or voiceover any time from your library — it's gone immediately. If you cancel a paid subscription, your saved data is permanently deleted 10 business days after cancellation, after email notices on day 0, day 5, and day 10. After deletion there are no recoverable copies. Full details are in our Terms.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reply to any subscription email, or contact us through clickprompt.app.
